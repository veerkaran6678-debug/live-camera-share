🔒 Live Camera Share

Share a live camera only when the person holding that camera chooses to start sharing and grants browser permission.

Privacy: The camera owner must tap “Start camera and share.” Stop sharing at any time. Only share your room ID with the intended viewer. This demo uses a public PeerJS signaling service and is not designed for sensitive or confidential video.

1. Camera owner

Open this page on the phone that will share its camera. Tap the button and allow camera access. Then send the room ID to the person you want to watch.

2. Viewer

On the viewer's device, enter the room ID given by the camera owner and tap Connect. The camera owner must already be sharing.

Status: Ready. Internet connection required.

This page must be hosted on HTTPS (for example, GitHub Pages). It uses PeerJS's public signaling service; camera access is requested only after the camera owner taps Start.
