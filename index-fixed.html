🔒 Live Camera Share

Camera sharing starts only when the camera owner chooses to start and grants permission.

Privacy: The camera owner must tap Start and may stop at any time. Share the room ID only with the intended viewer. This demo uses public PeerJS signaling and is not suitable for sensitive video.

1. Camera owner

On the phone sharing its camera, tap Start and allow camera access. Send the room ID to the intended viewer.

2. Viewer

On the viewer's phone, enter the room ID from the camera owner and tap Connect. The owner must already be sharing.

Status: Ready. Internet connection required.

Must be hosted on HTTPS. Some networks block direct WebRTC connections. This is a simple demo, not a secure production service.
